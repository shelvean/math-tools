1

Is this an ordinary or a partial differential equation?

An ODE has derivatives with respect to one independent variable. A PDE has partial derivatives with respect to two or more.

2

What is the order?

The order equals the highest derivative present.

3

Is it linear or nonlinear?

An ODE is linear if \(y\) and all its derivatives appear only to the first power, are never multiplied together, and are not inside nonlinear functions.

4

Is the ODE autonomous or non-autonomous?

An ODE is autonomous if the independent variable does not appear explicitly — only \(y\) and its derivatives determine the rate of change.

5

Is the linear ODE homogeneous or nonhomogeneous?

A linear ODE is homogeneous if every term contains \(y\) or one of its derivatives — no term depends only on the independent variable.
