Multi-Pendulum Chaos Simulator

This simulator models double and triple pendulums — classic examples of deterministic chaos. The equations of motion are derived from the Lagrangian and integrated using a 4th-order Runge-Kutta method.

Even the tiniest change in initial conditions (the perturbation) causes the trajectories to diverge exponentially — the hallmark of chaos. Enable perturbation to see two nearly identical pendulums trace wildly different paths.

The trail color can encode the speed of the bottom bob: blue = slow, red = fast.

Try It

  • Change the pendulum type between double and triple
  • Modify lengths, masses, and initial angles
  • Enable perturbation to visualize sensitivity to initial conditions
  • Experiment with trail styles: speed color, solid, or fading
