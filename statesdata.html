Top 15 states

Texas alone accounts for nearly two fifths of all U.S. users. Beyond that, the distribution spreads across major university hubs — California, New York, Virginia, Illinois, Massachusetts — suggesting discovery primarily through academic networks and course links.

All states at a glance

Every U.S. state, plus the District of Columbia, has at least one active user. Engagement remains highest in states with large public university systems, while Alaska sits at the tail with a single visitor this period.
