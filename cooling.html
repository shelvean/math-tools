Footnotes

  1. Newton's Law of Cooling states that the rate of temperature change is proportional to the difference between the object temperature and the ambient temperature: \(\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - T_{\text{env}})\), where \(k > 0\) is the cooling constant. ↑
  2. The Improved Euler (Heun's) method is a second-order Runge-Kutta scheme: it first predicts \(T^* = T_n + \Delta t\,f(T_n)\), then corrects with \(T_{n+1} = T_n + \frac{\Delta t}{2}[f(T_n) + f(T^*)]\). ↑
  3. The analytical (exact) solution is \(T(t) = T_{\text{env}} + (T_0 - T_{\text{env}})\,e^{-kt}\), obtained by separation of variables. ↑
