Business Math

Finance, linear systems, and two-variable optimization for business applications.

This collection covers a range of topics relevant to business mathematics, from financial calculations to matrix operations and optimization.

Finance

  • TVM Solver — compute present value, future value, payments, interest rates, and number of periods with amortization schedules

Linear Algebra for Business

  • Matrix Product — interactive matrix multiplication
  • Linear Equation Solver — solve a linear system with step-by-step row reduction
  • REF Solver — step-by-step Gaussian elimination to row echelon form
  • RREF Solver — step-by-step Gauss-Jordan elimination
  • Geometry of Linear Systems — visualize row and column pictures for 2D systems

Optimization

  • Method of Corners — visualize feasible regions and find optimal solutions for 2-variable linear programs
