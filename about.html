About

Shelvean Kapita · Instructional Assistant Professor, Texas A&M University

This site hosts a collection of browser-based, dependency-free mathematical tools developed for the courses I teach at Texas A&M University — primarily differential equations, linear algebra, and numerical methods. Each tool is written in plain HTML, JavaScript, and MathJax, runs entirely in the browser, and is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.

The goal is pedagogical transparency: to let students manipulate the mathematical structures behind a method and see the consequences immediately, without installing software or creating accounts. Instructors are welcome to use or adapt any of the tools in their own classrooms.

Between January 1, 2025 and April 7, 2026, the site recorded roughly 113,000 page views and 6,880 active users from 115 countries (see figure below). The tools have been used in instruction at institutions including Brown University, and comments from Gilbert Strang (MIT) and Steven Strogatz (Cornell) have been encouraging.
