A small site, a wide world

A small collection of browser-based mathematics tools has quietly become a study aid in 118 countries. This is what its first sixteen months of use look like. For a closer look at usage within the United States, see the state-by-state breakdown.

Reach by region

The site reaches every inhabited continent. North America leads on raw volume, but per-capita engagement is highest in small, English-fluent regions where access to commercial mathematics software is uneven.
