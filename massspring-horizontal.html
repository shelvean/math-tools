About This Simulation

This interactive tool visualizes a coupled horizontal mass-spring-damper system. Identical masses are connected by identical springs to each other and to fixed walls on either side.

where is the tridiagonal stiffness matrix coupling the masses through the springs.

Normal modes: The coupled system exhibits N distinct normal modes of oscillation. For 2 masses:

  • In-phase mode (): both masses move together
  • Out-of-phase mode (): masses move in opposite directions

Try setting equal initial displacements to excite the in-phase mode, or opposite displacements for the out-of-phase mode. Unequal initial conditions produce beats—a periodic exchange of energy between the masses.

Numerical method: The simulation uses the velocity Verlet (Störmer) integrator, a symplectic method that conserves energy well in undamped cases.
