Horizontal Coupled Mass-Spring System

This interactive tool visualizes a coupled horizontal mass-spring-damper system. Identical masses are connected by identical springs to each other and to fixed walls on either side.

\[ M\ddot{\mathbf{x}} + C\dot{\mathbf{x}} + K\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0} \]

where \(K\) is the tridiagonal stiffness matrix coupling the masses through the springs.

Normal Modes

The coupled system exhibits N distinct normal modes of oscillation. For 2 masses:

  • In-phase mode (\(\omega_1 = \sqrt{k/m}\)): both masses move together
  • Out-of-phase mode (\(\omega_2 = \sqrt{3k/m}\)): masses move in opposite directions

Try setting equal initial displacements to excite the in-phase mode, or opposite displacements for the out-of-phase mode. Unequal initial conditions produce beats -- a periodic exchange of energy between the masses.

Numerical Method

The simulation uses the velocity Verlet (Stormer) integrator, a symplectic method that conserves energy well in undamped cases.

\[ M\ddot{\mathbf{x}} + C\dot{\mathbf{x}} + K\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}, \quad \mathbf{x}(0) = \mathbf{x}_0, \quad \dot{\mathbf{x}}(0) = \mathbf{v}_0 \]

x1(t) -- Mass 1

x2(t) -- Mass 2

x3(t) -- Mass 3
