Optimization

Interactive optimization tools — from graphical linear programming (feasible regions and the method of corners) to continuous minimization with gradient descent and Newton's method.

Interactive tools for exploring optimization — both linear programming and continuous (unconstrained) minimization.

Graphical Methods

  • Method of Corners — visualize the feasible region, identify corner points, and find the optimal solution for 2-variable linear programs

Continuous Optimization

  • Quadratic Minimization — minimize an unconstrained convex quadratic with gradient descent or Newton's method; visualize iterates in 3D, step sizes, a user-defined stopping tolerance, and convergence
