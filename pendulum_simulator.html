Damped Pendulum Simulator

This interactive simulator models a damped simple pendulum governed by the nonlinear second-order differential equation:

\[ \ddot{\theta} + \dfrac{b}{mL}\dot{\theta} + \dfrac{g}{L}\sin(\theta) = 0 \]

with initial conditions \(\theta(0) = \theta_0\) and \(\dot{\theta}(0) = \omega_0\).

Visualizations

  • Pendulum animation: Shows the physical motion of the pendulum bob
  • Phase space: Plots angular velocity vs angle, revealing the system's qualitative behavior
  • Time series: Shows angular displacement over time

Numerical Methods

RK4 by default; switches to backward Euler for stiff overdamped cases.

Use the End Time (T) control to set simulation duration and time axis scaling. Inputs accept expressions like pi/4, 2*pi, etc.
