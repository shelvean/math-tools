Coupled Pendula with Spring Coupling

Two pendulums are coupled by a spring whose natural length equals the pivot separation. The spring exerts a horizontal restoring force proportional to the difference in bob displacements.

\(\ddot{\theta}_i = -\dfrac{g}{L_i}\sin\theta_i - \dfrac{b}{m_i L_i}\dot{\theta}_i + \dfrac{k_c}{m_i L_i}\cos\theta_i \cdot \Delta x\)

where \(\Delta x = L_2\sin\theta_2 - L_1\sin\theta_1\) is the spring extension, with opposite sign for each pendulum.

Observable Phenomena

  • Normal modes: In-phase (both swing together) and anti-phase (opposite directions)
  • Beats: Periodic energy transfer between pendulums when one is displaced
  • Synchronization: Damping drives the system toward the lower-energy mode
  • Symmetry breaking: Unequal lengths or masses prevent complete energy transfer

Normal Mode Frequencies

For identical pendulums (\(L_1 = L_2 = L\), \(m_1 = m_2 = m\)):

In-phase: \(\omega_+ = \sqrt{g/L}\)
Anti-phase: \(\omega_- = \sqrt{g/L + 2k_c/m}\)

The beat frequency is \(\omega_{\text{beat}} = (\omega_- - \omega_+)/2\), governing how fast energy transfers between pendulums.

Lagrangian Formulation

\( \textcolor{midnightblue}{\textbf{2 coupled pendulums:}} \)

\( \textcolor{maroon}{\mathcal{L} = \dfrac{1}{2}m_1 L_1^2 \dot{\theta}_1^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}m_2 L_2^2 \dot{\theta}_2^2 + m_1 g L_1 \cos\theta_1 + m_2 g L_2 \cos\theta_2 - \dfrac{k_c}{2}(L_2\sin\theta_2 - L_1\sin\theta_1)^2} \)

\( \textcolor{midnightblue}{\textbf{3 coupled pendulums:}} \)

\( \textcolor{maroon}{\mathcal{L} = \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left[\dfrac{1}{2}m_i L_i^2 \dot{\theta}_i^2 + m_i g L_i \cos\theta_i\right] - \dfrac{k_c}{2}\left[(L_2\sin\theta_2 - L_1\sin\theta_1)^2 + (L_3\sin\theta_3 - L_2\sin\theta_2)^2\right]} \)

Numerical Method

The simulation integrates the coupled 4D system using the classical 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) method, providing excellent accuracy for nonlinear oscillatory dynamics.
