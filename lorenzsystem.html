Lorenz System

\[ \begin{cases} \dot{x} = \sigma(y - x) \\ \dot{y} = x(\rho - z) - y \\ \dot{z} = xy - \beta z \end{cases} \]
Sensitive Dependence on Initial Conditions:
Trajectory 1: \(\mathbf{x}_1(0) = (x_0,\; y_0,\; z_0)\)
Trajectory 2: \(\mathbf{x}_2(0) = (x_0 + \varepsilon,\; y_0,\; z_0)\)
Both trajectories satisfy the same Lorenz system. A perturbation of \(\varepsilon\) in the initial \(x\)-coordinate leads to exponential divergence: \(\|\mathbf{x}_1(t) - \mathbf{x}_2(t)\| \sim \varepsilon\, e^{\lambda_1 t}\).
