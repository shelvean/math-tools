◆ Recent News & Upcoming Events

March 31, 2026

Awarded a $5,000 Provost OER Grant from the Provost's Office and University Libraries for creating an open-access Math 308 (Differential Equations) question bank, supporting free and equitable course materials for students.

April 30, 2026 · 11:15 a.m. · Rudder Tower

I gave a talk, Building Trust Through Transparency: Teaching mathematics in the browser — no black boxes, no installs, no barriers, at the TTLC 2026 conference, whose theme this year was Teaching and Learning with Integrity. The two-day event featured a keynote and workshop by Tricia Bertram Gallant, participant-driven plenary sessions, concurrent presentations by Texas A&M faculty, and a showcase of recent recipients of the university's highest teaching excellence honors.
